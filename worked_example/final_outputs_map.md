## What this tells you

- **Governance is the largest layer by file count** (79 files) but moderate in line count. Many small focused nodes.
- **TechSpec is the largest by line count** (6500). One canonical SDD + plans + deployment.
- **Producto and Panorama are surprisingly compact** (4100 combined). The "what" of a product is concise when you separate from technical "how".
- **Reference is highly heterogeneous** in content — small markdown READMEs + big Excel/PDF.

For a new project of similar complexity, expect similar totals. Smaller projects can scale down each layer proportionally.
